🎲 TOGEL GENERATOR (P2 & P6)

Rumus Mati 2D | ML MB INDX TY | Turun 1 Angka | Final BBFS + Jitu 432D

📜 PANUTAN (Referensi Rumus) ▼
🔹 Angka Mati = (AS&KOP P2) - (AS&KOP P6)
🔹 ML,MB,INDX,TY mapping per digit angka mati
🔹 BBFS ON 2D = gabung mapping, unik, lalu turun 1 angka + hapus angka mati
🔹 Mencari BBFS = (AS&KOP P6)*(Kepala&Ekor P6)*12 / (4D P6) → unik → hapus angka mati
🔹 JITU 432D = (AS&KOP P6)*(Kepala&Ekor P6)*12 → unik → hapus angka mati
🔹 Paling Kuat = irisan BBFS ON 2D & Final Angka Main & JITU 432D
🔹 Shot 432D = gabungan unik BBFS ON 2D + Final Angka Main + JITU 432D
🔹 2D Kuat = BBFS ON 2D (hasil akhir)
